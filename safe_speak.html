Our Features

🛡️ Anonymous

🌍 Multilingual

🔒 Safety Checks

🚨 Crisis Detection

🌙 Dark Mode

Switch between light and dark themes for a comfortable experience.

🚨 Safety First

Safe Speak is designed as a peer-support platform. It is not a replacement for professional medical, psychological or emergency services.


  • Do not share passwords or financial information.
  • Do not share your home address.
  • Report threatening or abusive behavior.
  • Seek professional help when necessary.
